Core capabilities

One system, two connected experiences.

The proof of concept demonstrates the complete journey from trainer onboarding and prediction to member workout execution and progress feedback.

🧠

Intelligent recommendation

Predict an appropriate workout category and display confidence, alternative probabilities and the factors influencing the result.

⚙️

Rule-based plan generation

Generate weekly plans using goal, availability, experience, equipment and physical restrictions.

📊

Progress monitoring

Track adherence, workout completion, calories, heart-rate trends, body measurements and projected progress.

🧑‍🏫

Trainer workspace

Register members, record fitness information, review predictions, assign plans and respond to alerts.

📱

Member mobile experience

View recommendations, follow guided sessions, complete workouts, review history and receive reminders.

🔐

Responsible data handling

Clear role separation, privacy settings, security controls and a non-medical recommendation disclaimer.

System flow

From fitness profile to measurable progress.

1

Register

Trainer creates the member account and captures fitness information.

2

Predict

The model recommends a suitable workout category.

3

Generate

Rules transform goals and restrictions into a weekly plan.

4

Perform

The member follows sessions and records completion.

5

Monitor

Progress dashboards inform trainer adjustments.

User roles

Designed around the responsibilities of each user.

Trainer web application

Manage, recommend and monitor.

Register and maintain member profilesRun and review workout-type predictionsGenerate and assign weekly workout plansRecord measurements and monitor progressReview alerts, adherence and milestones
Member mobile application

Follow, complete and improve.

Access the predicted workout categoryView the weekly plan and trainer notesFollow exercise details and active timersSave completion feedback and workout ratingsTrack history, badges and projected progress
